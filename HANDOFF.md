# Silver Glider Events — Master Reference

**Last updated:** September 9, 2026

## 1. What it is
Silver Glider Events is a lightweight tool for creating beautiful event pages, collecting RSVPs, linking guests to third-party ticket providers, and sending reminders. It is **Version 1** of a bigger platform, built for independent hosts, promoters, artists, venues, and private gatherings.

- **Live site:** https://silvergliderevents.com (custom domain; the Railway URL https://silver-glider-events-production.up.railway.app still serves the same app)
- **Silver Glider Events does not process ticket payments.** Hosts can publish Free RSVP events, use external ticket links, or select a feature-gated Silver Glider Commerce handoff. Checkout, inventory, orders, issued tickets, verification, refunds, payouts, and Stripe Connect belong exclusively to the separate Commerce service.

## 2. How to use it (organizer)
1. Go to the site and click **Create your event** → enter your email.
2. You get a **magic-link** email ("sign-in link"). Click it — no password.
3. You're taken to your **Dashboard**. From there: **Create Event**.
4. Fill in title, date, time, and the unified **Location** search. Google Places accepts establishments and normal street/residential addresses; address-only locations may add an optional friendly Location Name, and manual entry remains available. Then choose a presentation:
   - **Standard** uses a cover image, full event details, and an optional gradient/effect background. **Choose image** opens the local uploader; **Browse free photos** opens Unsplash. The picker separates 4 **Gradients** (**Midnight**, **Aurora**, **Sunset**, **Ocean**) from the ordered **Effects** (**Halloween**, **The Last Guest**, **Disco**, **Fog**, **Kraft paper**, **TV static**, **Liquid Stardust**, **Color Static**, **After Hours Saloon**, **Match Photo**). Match Photo derives a dark, readable palette from the selected artwork. Moving effects loop with poster, reduced-motion, and data-saving fallbacks.
   - **Flyer** accepts one uncropped poster/flyer upload and renders an isolated, centered, poster-first public page. An optional designer name and Instagram handle appear only after a flyer exists; the subtle public credit prefers the normalized Instagram handle. The default Flyer backdrop is the fixed, darkened plaster-wall image at `public/images/flyer-plaster-wall.jpg`, while an explicitly selected effect overrides it. The normal RSVP, external-ticket, guest-list, comments, host, calendar, privacy, and organizer-management systems are reused unchanged.
   - The Unsplash picker opens on **Halloween** and includes separate **Fall**, **Silver Glider Picks**, and supporting visual collections. Search runs only when a pill, Enter, or Search is selected and uses a bounded server cache to reduce API requests.
   - An optional **Presented by** name. The first one creates a reusable public host page at `/h/<slug>`; future events automatically reuse it. **Settings** controls the host name, unique slug, large header image, logo/avatar, bio, Instagram handle, and website URL. The signed-in account email remains the only email field until a separate Booking Email feature is designed.
   - Description, category, capacity, admission, and visibility. Admission supports Free RSVP, External tickets with the established displayed price and optional outbound URL, and a feature-gated Sell tickets with Silver Glider handoff backed only by a Commerce event reference. **Public** and **Private — Link Only** events can independently enable a first-name/avatar guest list, one named +1 per RSVP, and text-only attendee comments under **Guest experience**. Private events stay off public host pages, discovery, and The Line while remaining accessible to anyone with the direct link. **Secret Show Mode** remains private-only and adds a six-character access-code gate; the Create Event banner is only a shortcut into those existing settings.
   - Mobile keeps the original single-column creation flow. On desktop, create/edit becomes a wide two-column workspace with **Page Design** beside **Event Information**; the Secret Show prompt spans the workspace. Standard background options stay visible after a cover is selected, so hosts can choose imagery before or after choosing the page background.
5. **Publish.** You get a shareable link and a mobile-first public page. The owner-only **Edit event** control opens a right-side rail on desktop and a collapsible bottom sheet on mobile. Image, background, details, location, flyer credit, and guest-setting changes preview immediately but persist only after **Save changes**; Cancel restores the saved event. When a published event with confirmed RSVPs changes date, start time, or location, both editors summarize the exact change and ask the host to **Save & notify** or **Save without email**. QR download remains an organizer promotion tool and is not shown on the public event page.
6. Manage the event through a focused action bar: **Copy event link**, **View page**, and **Edit** stay visible; **Duplicate** and **Cancel** live under the clearly labeled **Event actions** menu. Duplicate shows an immediate working state, prevents repeat requests, and opens the new draft directly in the editor; failures restore the action and show an error. Cancelling asks whether to email every confirmed RSVP, and the management page shows the latest update/cancellation delivery status. Desktop management pairs large event artwork with a structured right-side action panel, then gives the guest list the full width below. **Promote your event** uses descriptive action rows for sharing, QR download, previous-guest invitations, and follower invitations plus a visually separate editorial opportunity for **The Line**. **Invite previous guests** lets a host choose one owned past event, search/review/uncheck eligible people, and send one artwork-led invitation batch to an upcoming public or private event. Only confirmed primary RSVPs who opted into future emails from that host are eligible; named guests, cancelled RSVPs, host opt-outs, existing target attendees, and already-notified recipients are excluded. **Export CSV** stays with guest search. To declutter the event list, **swipe an event left to Archive** (reversible — guest data always kept); permanent delete lives at the bottom of the manage page behind a double confirm.
7. **Submit to The Line** to request a feature in the Silver Glider SMS/marketing channel (an admin reviews it).
8. Use the quiet **Feedback** button in the bottom-right of any signed-in app page to report a bug, suggestion, or other feedback. Reports automatically include the authenticated host, page, related event when applicable, and browser context.

**You stay logged in for 30 days** on your device (and it auto-extends while you're active), so you won't need a new email every time.

## 3. How guests use it
- Open the event link → tap **RSVP**. On mobile, the RSVP action docks at the bottom until the inline action/form is reached.
- The compact form asks for name first and progressively reveals email; phone and host-update consent remain under **More options**. Consent explicitly covers future invitations from that host and can be withdrawn from any such email. Guests do not create an account, and the expanded RSVP form can be collapsed.
- They get an artwork-led **confirmation email with a calendar invite (.ics)** and can add it to their calendar. The email keeps Silver Glider quiet, leads with uncropped event artwork, then shows the event title, optional linked host, optional **Listen here**, available date/time/venue details, and the event CTA. Missing content is omitted rather than shown as an empty placeholder.
- They receive **reminder emails** the day before (4pm) and day of (9am), in the event's time zone.
- If the host chooses to notify after changing the date, start time, or location—or after cancelling—the primary email on every confirmed RSVP receives a one-way transactional notice even when ordinary reminders were declined. Update and cancellation messages include the attendee's secure event link and a versioned calendar attachment. Named guest emails are not contacted separately in V1.
- If the event hits capacity, RSVPs stop and it shows "Event full."
- Guests can cancel their RSVP from a link in their email (which frees a spot).
- The on-page success message is **“Your RSVP is confirmed.”**
- When a private host enables **Allow guests**, an RSVP can include one named guest with an optional email. Capacity and the displayed attendance total count both the attendee and guest.
- When enabled, the public guest list shows first names only. A fresh RSVP unlocks the comment wall immediately on that browser; the confirmation-email link restores attendee access on another or returning device. Attendees can post or delete their own 300-character comments, while the event host can delete any comment. Existing-email attempts never grant access based on email knowledge alone.
- Signed-in RSVP forms reuse the saved account name and email. When identity is verified by the signed session or the attendee's private manage token, the saved personal RSVP photo appears in the guest list; arbitrary email entry cannot claim another account's image.
- Guest-list previews show first names beneath avatars for one to five attendees. At six or more, they switch to compact overlapping circles; **See everyone** opens the accessible desktop modal or expands the full list inline on mobile.
- Secret Shows reveal no title, date, venue, description, metadata, or other event details until the correct code is entered. A signed, time-limited browser cookie remembers the unlock; afterward the guest sees the normal private event page and all enabled RSVP, guest-list, named-guest, comment, share, and calendar features.

## 5. Login & security (plain English)
- Login is by **magic link** (email). No passwords ever.
- After the first login, a secure **30-day session** keeps you signed in on that browser; it auto-refreshes while you're active.
- Signing out clears it. Requesting too many magic links too fast is rate-limited.
- Public event pages are open to everyone; the organizer dashboard is protected. Secret Shows are private link-only events with a separately stored scrypt code hash, signed versioned unlock cookies, and per-session/per-IP attempt limits. Replacing the code invalidates prior unlocks.
- Public RSVP submissions and confirmation-email resend attempts are rate-limited to reduce spam and fake capacity filling.
- Host/admin-rendered text is escaped before insertion into HTML, and executable URL schemes are rejected. Database IDs from the authenticated session—not editable names or emails—are the authorization source of truth.

## 6. Tech summary (for a developer)
- **Stack:** Node.js + Express 5, PostgreSQL, server-rendered HTML + vanilla JS. No build step, no framework.
- **Repo (local):** `~/silver-glider-events`
- **Entry point:** `src/index.js`
- **Key folders:** `src/routes/` (auth, events, public event/RSVP flows, isolated public host pages, uploads, photos, admin), `src/lib/` (mailer, server-only Twilio SMS transport, session, shared public HTML, calendar/ics, unsplash, cloudinary, slug, csv), `src/jobs/reminders.js` (scheduled reminders), `src/jobs/event-notifications.js` (critical update/cancellation delivery and retry), `src/jobs/previous-guest-invitations.js` (reviewed past-guest invitation delivery and retry), `src/views/` (HTML pages), `public/` (CSS + JS/assets).
- **Database:** auto-migrations run on startup from `src/db/migrations/*.sql`. Core tables include organizers, magic_link_tokens, events, event_secret_codes, rsvps, event_comments, message_log, event_notification_batches, previous_guest_invitation_batches, line_submissions, feedback_submissions, host_invitations, host_follows, event_photos, and commerce_feature_interests.
- **Current migrations:** `001` through `029_previous_guest_invitations.sql`.
- **Health check:** `GET /health` returns `{status:"ok", version:"...", sha:"..."}`.
- **Release version:** `package.json` is the canonical semantic version, production commits receive matching annotated Git tags, and `CHANGELOG.md` records user-facing releases. `/health` returns both `version` and `sha` for deployment verification.

## 8. How to deploy a change
Production may contain promoted events, so the MVP workflow is **local first, production second**:

1. Run and test the change locally at `http://localhost:3100` against `postgresql://localhost:5432/sge_dev`.
2. Keep `NODE_ENV=development` and `REMINDERS_ENABLED=false` locally. Never use the Railway production database for development or test data.
   Local cover uploads automatically go to `sg-events-dev/covers`; production covers remain in `sg-events/covers`.
3. Verify `/health` and the specific organizer/guest flows affected by the change.
4. Commit only after local testing passes. Push when GitHub credentials are available.
5. Before deploying, explicitly report whether the release affects existing live event pages, shared Cloudinary assets, guest lists/RSVPs, outgoing emails/reminders, or only new/local behavior. Replacing an asset at an existing Cloudinary URL affects every live event using it.
6. Deploy to production from `~/silver-glider-events`:

```
git rev-parse --short HEAD > .git-sha && railway up --service silver-glider-events
```

7. Verify `curl https://silver-glider-events-production.up.railway.app/health`. The `version` should match `package.json`, and the `sha` should match `git rev-parse --short HEAD`. Then perform a small, non-destructive production smoke test. (`.git-sha` stays a tracked-but-modified file each deploy — that's expected.)

**Current machine note:** GitHub CLI authentication is active for `code666now` and Git operations use HTTPS. If a future push fails, check `gh auth status` before changing the remote. This remains separate from Railway: a successful GitHub push does not deploy production, and a successful Railway deployment does not prove the repository backup is current.

**Commerce admission foundation (September 3, 2026):** migration `024_commerce_admission_types.sql` adds nullable `events.commerce_event_id` and accepts canonical `free_rsvp`, `external_tickets`, and `silver_glider_tickets` values while retaining legacy admission values. The editor exposes Free RSVP, External tickets, then Sell tickets with Silver Glider as a readable **Coming soon** choice until `COMMERCE_ENABLED=true` and a Commerce base URL are configured. The badge disappears automatically when enabled. Events stores no authoritative Commerce price, inventory, availability, order, payment, ticket, verification, refund, payout, or Stripe Connect data. Duplicating a native ticketed event deliberately drops the Commerce reference and leaves the copy as a draft so one Commerce product cannot be sold by two Events records accidentally.

**Ticketing launch interest (September 3, 2026):** migration `025_commerce_feature_interest.sql` adds a separate `commerce_feature_interests` table for explicit, reversible, one-time Commerce launch consent. Signed-in hosts see a compact **Notify me** action under the Coming soon admission choice; no duplicate identity form is shown because the authenticated organizer email is reused. Opting in twice remains one row, and **Remove me** immediately excludes the host. Super Admins use `/admin/ticketing` to review interested, awaiting, and notified hosts and to send a private test email. The launch button stays locked until Commerce is fully configured and requires an explicit confirmation. Atomic delivery claims, stale-claim recovery, success timestamps, and per-recipient errors make retries safe without emailing a delivered host twice. Nothing sends automatically, and RSVP updates, Host follows, and general marketing consent remain independent.

**Follow Host V1 (implemented August 12):** `organizers` remains the production identity table, but rows are now treated as shared email identities rather than automatically as creators. Public Host Pages still require `org_name` and `public_slug`; following never fills those fields or creates a Host Page. Migration `020_follow_host_v1.sql` adds trusted intent/target/return metadata to one-time magic links and a reactivatable `host_follows` relationship. Signed-in users follow immediately. Signed-out users enter only an email, receive the normal one-time 15-minute magic link, complete the follow transaction, receive the existing 30-day session, and return to the Host Page. No separate welcome email is sent. `/following` lists followed public Host Pages and counts published public upcoming events. RSVP records remain unauthenticated guest records, and existing RSVP `organizer_optin` announcement recipients remain a separate consent system for V1. When a signed-in Host views their own public Host Page, the server renders one quiet `← Dashboard` hero control; public and other signed-in visitors receive no owner-control markup.

**Photo picker (developer note):** Category presets live in `public/js/event-form.js`. **Halloween** is the initial collection, followed by **Fall**, **Silver Glider Picks**, and supporting visual categories. Each pill issues one tuned Unsplash search only when the user selects the pill, presses Enter, or uses Search; typing alone does not call the API. `/api/photos/search` accepts a capped `per_page` value (1–24). `src/lib/unsplash.js` caches search responses in memory for 30 minutes and evicts the oldest entry once the cache reaches 200 keys. The cache is per server instance and does not require Redis at the current single-instance scale.

**Background system (developer note):** `events.background_theme` holds one key—an MVP gradient (`midnight`, `aurora`, `sunset`, `ocean`) or an effect (`halloween`, `last-guest`, `disco`, `fog`, `paper`, `static`, `liquid-stardust`, `color-static`, `saloon`, `adaptive`). `adaptive` is labeled **Match Photo** and derives the Standard page palette from its artwork. `violet` and `ember` are no longer offered or accepted for new saves, but `src/routes/public.js` retains legacy rendering support for already-published events. **Standard pages** use `.bg-theme .bg-<key>` and may receive an image-derived palette from `public/js/public-event.js`; choosing a cover does not hide the background controls. **Flyer pages** are isolated in `src/views/event-public-flyer.html` and `public/css/event-public-flyer.css`; without an explicit effect they use the fixed grayscale plaster photograph `public/images/flyer-plaster-wall.jpg` under a dark readability veil. Explicit effects render via `.event-bg.fx-<key>` and override the Flyer default. Kraft paper and After Hours Saloon are still images; TV static is a canvas; Halloween, The Last Guest, Disco, Fog, Liquid Stardust, and Color Static use optimized Cloudinary video loops. Liquid Stardust and Color Static add an end-to-start crossfade to hide source-video seams. Motion respects hidden tabs, autoplay failure, data-saving mode, and reduced-motion preferences. Adding an effect requires a whitelisted key, asset mapping, picker swatch, and fallback; no schema change.

**RSVP confirmation email (developer note):** `src/lib/mailer.js` has a confirmation-only 620px presentation-table layout; reminders and other shared emails keep their established layout. The small Silver Glider logo sits above full-width event art. Cloudinary landscape covers/flyers are delivered as Outlook-safe JPEGs on a 620×560 predominant-color padded canvas, preserving the original composition; portrait art keeps its aspect ratio. The shell remains fixed black with neutral dark cards. The primary CTA uses the saved artwork accent; secondary status, identity, vibe, and action elements use a brighter contrast-safe tint of the same hue with dynamically recolored, cacheable PNG icons. Missing or unusable accents fall back to Silver Glider teal. When no artwork exists, paper, disco, fog, and saloon may supply static poster imagery; email never embeds motion. Title and optional host/vibe identity sit above a details-only card whose date, time, venue, and maps rows are conditional. Sending, resends, `.ics` attachment, reminder timing, manage links, and event URLs are unchanged.
