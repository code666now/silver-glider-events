# Getting Home: public San Francisco venues

This is Commit 1 only. Eligible published public events at an exact San Francisco street address selected in Google Places can receive the existing **Help me get home** dashboard action for confirmed Going guests. Pending, unmatched, unsupported or expired results keep **Get directions**. Make-Out Room still uses its original verified walks and preview. Manually entered addresses, private/hidden events, Secret Shows, ride texts and ride email links are outside the citywide rollout.

## Preparation and operating limits

- `TRANSIT_SF_GETTING_HOME_ENABLED=true` arms the Railway safety gate; it does not enable public city access by itself. A dedicated Super Admin must also turn on the setting at `/admin/transportation`. The rollout requires `TRANSIT_GETTING_HOME_ENABLED=true`, `TRANSIT_511_API_KEY` (or the legacy variable) and a server-only `GEOAPIFY_API_KEY`. No billing or other service needs enabling for the intended free pilot. Use a dedicated Geoapify project to give the app its own free-plan headroom. The app reserves at most 2,400 modeled one-origin-to-one-target credits per rolling 24 hours, including failed requests; provider limits and other uses can still make service unavailable. Keep both transit messaging flags off.
- A minute worker notices committed events and real address or event-time changes. It does not run in the publish request. The existing Postgres database protects work across instances, reuses successful walks for up to 24 hours and deduplicates the provider budget. Only the ready flag reaches the dashboard DTO; exact venue pins, stop coordinates used for checks and provider responses stay server-side.
- The Google selection identifies the event record. The walking origin comes from an exact [DataSF city address point](https://data.sf.gov/d/3mea-di5p), never from a Google geocode. Nearby 511 street boarding stops and BART entrances with a directional pathway to the platforms are Geoapify targets. A BART walk goes to the named entrance; the existing five-minute buffer includes time to reach the platform and board. There is no straight-line walk fallback. A station without a verified public entrance is omitted. Geoapify and OpenStreetMap credit appears beside the existing 511 attribution.
- The original pilot and the city have separate bounded feed caches so a city build failure does not stop the pilot. The city worker and planner share the Muni/BART snapshot; full trips are preserved for boardability and BART connections. If the city feed exceeds the 1.6 million retained-record cap, it fails closed. Venue walks and event readiness are independently checked against exact current event inputs. A stop that has materially moved forces recalculation; the event date and an actual scheduled ride or continuous overnight service must be viable before marking ready.
- Transportation analytics count opens, checked routes, explicit bus tiles, Uber and Lyft taps once per guest and event within the deduplication window. Returning guests are signed-in people who opened plans for two different events in the current 45-day measurement window. The preview does not count. Host and event summaries are aggregate and do not measure attendance or completed trips. Tracking does not activate distribution. Neither provider receives guest data. The walking provider receives public venue/stop coordinates only. No raw provider request or response is written to logs.

## QA before enabling

1. Run `npm run check` against the isolated `sge_test` database. The fixtures include a new street venue, missing/moved stops, after-midnight schedules, BART entrance access, new/changed events, privacy and the rolling budget.
2. In an approved test environment, measure a cold and refreshed regional feed import with both the pilot and city caches running. Confirm the archive fits the record limit and that memory, startup and request latency fit existing Railway headroom. Do not expand limits or add a paid service without approval.
3. Spot-check the actual provider walks for a downtown BART entrance, a bus-only venue and a venue next to a barrier or with no late service. Confirm the route list, last departure, next two, 5-minute buffer, and the false/expired fallback. Verify the secret/hidden/manual cases and that no emails or texts are sent. Only then arm the Railway gate and turn the rollout on in Transportation. Turn it off there to stop new city preparation and access without affecting Make-Out Room. A lookup already underway may finish.
