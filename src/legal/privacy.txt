Detailed Information on the Processing of Personal Data
Handling and Facilitating Payments for Event Access
Silver Glider Events facilitates transactions for Tickets and other paid Event Access between attendees and Hosts. Hosts who publish Events on Silver Glider Events act as the merchant of record for their respective sales of paid Event Access. Payments are processed securely through Stripe, Inc. on behalf of the Host. When you purchase Event Access, your payment information is transmitted directly to Stripe. Silver Glider Events receives transaction confirmation and associated Personal Data including your name, email address, and purchase details solely for issuing or managing Event Access, maintaining your account, and facilitating the transaction between you and the Host.

Stripe, Inc. · Place of processing: United States · Personal Data processed: email address, first name, last name, transaction data

Hosts may use third-party ticket providers for Tickets or other Event Access. Purchases and registrations made through those providers are governed by the applicable third party’s terms and privacy policy. Those providers independently determine how they process Personal Data, and Silver Glider Events receives such Data only when it is shared with the Platform or needed to provide connected services.

Event Access, Ticket Issuance, and Activity History
When you purchase a Ticket, RSVP, register, join a guest list or waitlist, accept an invitation, or make a reservation through Silver Glider Events, an Event Access record is generated. That record may include your name, email address, participation status, Event details, and account association. The Host will have access to relevant information for guest list management, event entry, attendee communication, and administration of the Event. Silver Glider Events retains paid Event Access purchase history for a minimum of 7 years for accounting, legal compliance, and customer service purposes.

In the event of a refund or chargeback for paid Event Access, your transaction data may be shared between Silver Glider Events, the Host, Stripe, and any applicable third-party provider to process and resolve the transaction.

Event Host Application Data
When you submit a Request Access application to become an event host on Silver Glider Events, we collect your first name, last name, email address, venue or project name, city, and optionally your website or Instagram URL and a description of your shows. This data is used solely to review and process your application. Approved host application data is retained for the duration of the host relationship plus 3 years. Declined application data is retained for 12 months.

Public Host Profiles
When a host account is approved, a public profile is created on Silver Glider Events. This profile may include the host's name, venue or project name, city, and event listings. This information is publicly visible to all users of the platform. To request removal or modification of public profile information, contact contact@silverglidertickets.com.

Follower Relationships
Fans on Silver Glider Events may follow public profiles of artists, venues, and promoters. Following a public profile is a public action. Event hosts can see the number of followers on their profile. Individual follower identities are not publicly displayed but may be accessible to the host for the purposes of notifying followers of new events. Users may unfollow a profile at any time through their account settings.

SMS Communications and The Silver Glider Line
Silver Glider Events operates a telephone service called The Silver Glider Line (+1 (844) 261-6758) which provides a weekly recorded message featuring upcoming shows and events. No personal data is collected through inbound calls to The Silver Glider Line.

If you provide your phone number and consent to receive SMS communications from Silver Glider Events, you agree to receive text messages related to RSVP confirmations, reminders, event updates, Host announcements, event recommendations, ticket announcements, venue updates, presale alerts, platform updates, and promotional communications. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies.

You may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any SMS message. For help, reply HELP or contact contact@silverglidertickets.com. Silver Glider Entertainment Inc. will not share your phone number with third parties for marketing purposes without your explicit consent.

SMS consent is not a condition of obtaining Event Access or using the Platform.

Weekly Event Drop Email Subscription
Silver Glider Events offers a weekly email drop featuring upcoming shows in your city (San Francisco or Los Angeles). When you subscribe, we collect your email address and city preference. You may unsubscribe at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in any email or by contacting contact@silverglidertickets.com. Your email address will be removed from the subscription list within 10 business days of your request.

Cookie Policy
Silver Glider Events uses Trackers including Cookies. Cookies are small sets of data stored in the User's browser used to track usage, maintain sessions, and improve the service. Users may control cookie preferences through their browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect the functionality of certain features of the platform.
